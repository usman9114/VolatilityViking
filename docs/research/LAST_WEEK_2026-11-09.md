## Reported causes (press, not verified against primary data)
- **ETF outflows:** about $1.9B net out of US spot Bitcoin ETFs over seven sessions, 5–13 Nov (Farside, via press).
- **Forced liquidations:** about $1.2B on 13 Nov and $1.9–2B within 24 hours over the weekend.
- **Macro:** a stronger dollar (DXY above 103), hotter-than-expected US CPI, rising yields, and a broader tech selloff. The December Fed meeting is flagged as the next catalyst.
- BTC's sixth straight weekly loss. Some single-source explanations are speculative.

## 2. BTC since 1 Jan 2025: what perfect hindsight would have earned

| Approach | Return |
|---|---|
| Just hold | -18% |
| Sell at the exact top (06 Oct 2025), buy back at the exact bottom (30 Jun 2026) | +74% |
| Same, but buy back at the bottom with 2x leverage ("double down") | +116% |
| Know every day in advance whether it goes up or down | +17,059% (172x money) |

These need to know the exact top and bottom in advance. Nobody can; the question is what rules that only see the past would have done.

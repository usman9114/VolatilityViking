## Method notes

Gate criteria use the pre-registered-cost run, except the Sharpe CI (stress run, as pre-registered). Perp variants take signals from spot closes, are zeroed before the perp lists, and a coin with no bar at an execution instant is held (counted as a coverage warning). Execution is the first hourly instant after the daily close (perp open, spot close) with 5 bp slippage and taker fees.
